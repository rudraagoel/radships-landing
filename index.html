how it works

four steps.

pick a theme

A broad new prompt lands every Sunday. Interpret it any way you like.

02

Build an app, game, website, bot, tool, API, or anything else that runs as software.

03

Everything you need to know before you start shipping.

how does RadShips work?+

RadShips lasts 12 weeks. A new theme drops each Sunday. Build software around it through the week, then ship, submit, and log your hours by Friday. After review, approved hours become RadTokens. On Sunday, a new theme starts the loop again.

what can I build?+

Anything that is software: apps, games, websites, tools, bots, APIs, browser extensions, and more. Build one big project or several small ones—there is no project limit.

do I need to finish every week?+

Only if you want RadTokens for that week. You are welcome to skip a theme or continue a late project, but it cannot earn RadShips credit after the Friday deadline.

can I use AI?+

A little AI help for debugging is okay. RadShips is about coding and learning, so the major work and understanding should be yours.

how do RadTokens work?+

When a shipped project and its logged hours are approved, those hours become RadTokens. Spend them on rewards in the RadShips shop.
