01 / THE BRIEF

TWELVE WEEKS
OF GOOD IDEAS.

RadShips is a 12-week You Ship, We Ship program under Hack Club. Every Sunday, a brand-new software theme lands in your inbox.

By Friday, make and ship a software project around it: an app, game, tool, website, or bot. Approved projects earn RadTokens based on the time you put in.

THE WEEK, IN DETAIL

Every one of the 12 weeks follows the same loop. You decide how ambitious the project is; the deadline keeps it moving.

  1. SUNDAY

    Pick a direction

    A broad theme drops. Read it, sketch an idea, and choose a software project that fits. One theme can mean completely different things to different people.

  2. MONDAY — THURSDAY

    Build at your pace

    Work on one larger project, several small ones, or quick experiments. There is no project limit and no required number of hours—just build something you can ship.

  3. FRIDAY

    Submit before the deadline

    Ship your completed software project, then log the hours you spent on it. When the weekly theme closes, new submissions for that theme are no longer eligible for RadTokens.

  4. AFTER REVIEW

    Turn approved work into rewards

    Once your hours are approved, they become RadTokens. Spend them in the RadShips Shop; new themed prizes can appear as the season progresses, while previous rewards remain available.

04 / FAQ

GOOD
QUESTIONS.

What can I build?+

Anything and however many software projects you like: apps, games, websites, tools, bots, and more. Each project must be shipped by Friday, with its hours logged, to count for that week.

Do I need to finish every week?+

Yes, if you want to redeem the hours for RadTokens. If you do not finish in time, you can take the project to any other YSWS that is willing to take it.

Can I use AI?+

No. A little AI help for debugging is allowed, but the major goal here is for people to actually code and learn.

How do RadTokens work?+

Finished, approved software projects earn RadTokens based on the hours you log. Spend them in the RadShips prize shop.
