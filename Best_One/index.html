03 / FAQ

GOOD
QUESTIONS.

How does RadShips work?+

RadShips runs for 12 weeks. Each Sunday, a new theme drops. From Monday through Thursday, build any software project inspired by it—solo or with friends. By Friday, ship a working version, submit it, and log the hours you spent. After review, approved hours become RadTokens that you can redeem in the RadShips shop. Then a new theme arrives and the loop starts again.

Each theme closes after Friday. A project finished later can still be continued or submitted to another YSWS if it qualifies there, but it cannot earn RadTokens for the closed RadShips week.

What can I build?+

Anything that is software: apps, games, websites, tools, bots, APIs, browser extensions, and more. Build one big project or several small ones. Whatever you make must be working, shipped, and logged by Friday to count for that week.

Do I need to finish every week?+

Only if you want to earn RadTokens for that week. You are welcome to skip a theme or keep working on a late project, but late work cannot receive RadShips credit once that Friday deadline has passed.

Can I use AI?+

Use AI sparingly for debugging or small bits of help. RadShips is about actually coding and learning, so the major work and understanding should be yours.

How do RadTokens work?+

Log the time you spent on your shipped software. If the project and hours are approved, those hours turn into RadTokens. Spend them on rewards in the RadShips shop.
